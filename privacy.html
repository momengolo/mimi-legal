Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the ChooseForMimi publishing tool ("the Service") handles information.

1. What we access

When we connect our own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, the platform gives the Service an access token. We use it only to upload and publish our own videos, and to read the status of those uploads and basic profile information (for example, our account name) for our own account.

2. What we collect

The Service does not collect personal information from visitors or viewers. It stores the access tokens and the videos and captions we upload, on systems we control or on our automation provider's systems.

3. How we use it

Only to publish and manage our own content. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Service providers

We may use third-party automation and hosting providers to run the Service. They process data only to operate it on our behalf.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep access tokens only while the connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok, Instagram or Google account settings under connected apps, and we will delete stored tokens on request.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
